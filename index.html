How the groups are decided, using the last 10 days unless noted. Push now: 4+ sold and click→order of 1.5% or more. Fading: sold 5+ in Oct 1–13, but sales per LIVE hour have since halved or worse. Give more airtime: CTR of 4%+ on 400+ impressions but 3 or fewer sold, or a strong click→order on little exposure. Swap out: 3,000+ impressions across both periods with at most 1 sold, or 1,500+ recent impressions, no sales and CTR under 2%. Hold: everything else, mostly small numbers.
